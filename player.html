About Manipur Lagao Khaoba

Traditional 3D Dice Game • Rules & Payout Structure
🖼️ Official Rules & Regulations Poster: Tiered 1x - 5x Profit

✨ Game Background

In Manipur, Lagao Khaoba is the premier traditional dice board game. Six special 6-sided dice are tumbled inside a conical woven bamboo basket (Thumok) onto a marked mat (Laiyum).

Traditional 2+ Matching Dice Payout Rule:

Winning strictly starts when at least two (2) of the same dice land on your chosen symbol. A single die landing is a loss:

• 0 Dice: Lose (Admin takes the money)

• 1 Die: Lose (Admin takes the money)

• 2 Dice: Win 1x bet profit + get your original bet back (2x Total Return)

• 3 Dice: Win 2x bet profit + get your original bet back (3x Total Return)

• 4 Dice: Win 3x bet profit + get your original bet back (4x Total Return)

• 5 Dice: Win 4x bet profit + get your original bet back (5x Total Return)

• 6 Dice: Win 5x bet profit + get your original bet back (6x Total Return)

📌 Mandatory Requirement: A minimum of 6 players is required to start any game round!

The 6 3D Dice Symbols:

  • Jhandi (Flag): 3D waving red banner on a golden pole.
  • Munda (Crown): 3D jeweled royal golden crown ("Mapugidi Munda").
  • Hukhum (Spade): 3D chiseled obsidian spade with ridge lines.
  • Pan (Heart): 3D convex ruby crystal heart with specular gleam.
  • Itt (Diamond): 3D faceted crystal gemstone lozenge.
  • Chidi (Club): 3D emerald trefoil club with spherical lobes.
